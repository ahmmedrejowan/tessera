# Finding things

Browse is the library: every asset Tessera found inside every pack, or the packs themselves.

## Assets or packs

The switch at the top changes what the grid holds:

- **Assets**: every model, sprite, texture, sound, font and HDRI, each as one tile, whatever
  archive it lives in.
- **Packs**: the downloads themselves, with their covers, what they hold and their license.

## Search

The search box looks at file names, folder names, tags and every pack's details, inside archives
too. `⌘F` / `Ctrl+F` jumps to it from anywhere. Typing shows suggestions: pages, actions, packs,
collections and assets; Enter searches everything.

## Filters

Down the left, and they stack. Each shows how many things it would leave:

- **Type**: 3D models, sprites and 2D, textures, UI, sound effects, music, fonts, HDRIs, documents.
- **Format**: the file extensions actually present, such as GLB, FBX, PNG, OGG or TTF.
- **Source**: the sites and places your packs came from.
- **Creator**: who made them.
- **License**: what each allows.
- **Genre**, **style** and **tags**: as recorded on the pack.

**Clear** puts them all back. **Save as smart collection** turns what you are looking at into a
[collection](/docs/collections) that keeps itself up to date.

## Sorting

Best match, name, newest, size, pack or type. Without search words, best match is simply name.

## One asset, several formats

A model that ships as FBX, GLB and OBJ is one tile, with its formats listed. Tessera knows which
of them a game wants and copies the right one.

## The keyboard

- Arrow keys move the cursor through the grid.
- **Space** or **Enter** opens what the cursor is on in the viewer.
- **S** stars what is picked out, or what the cursor is on.
- `⌘A` / `Ctrl+A` picks everything the search matches, however many that is.
- **Esc** puts everything back.
- Click a tile to open it; hold (or right-click) to pick it out; `⌘`/`Ctrl` click adds to a
  selection, shift click takes a range.

## What you can do with a selection

Star it, add it to a collection, copy it into a game, or move it to the bin. The bar along the
bottom says how many are picked and what they come to.
