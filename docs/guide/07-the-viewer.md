# Looking closely

Press **Space** on any asset and the viewer opens over the grid. Nothing is unpacked: Tessera reads
what it needs out of the archive as it is.

## What each kind shows

- **Models** turn in 3D, with the triangle and vertex counts, the meshes, the materials and
  textures, the size in units, and any animations it carries. Every format the asset comes in is
  listed, and you can switch between them.
- **Images** zoom, with pixel art kept sharp rather than smoothed.
- **Textures** show the same way, with their size and what map they look like.
- **HDRIs** become a sky you can look around.
- **Sounds and music** play, with a waveform and the length.
- **Fonts** type your own sample text, with sliders for every axis a variable font has.
- **Documents**, including license files and readmes, are shown as text.

## Beside the picture

- **This file**: its name, format, size, and the folder it sits in, archive included.
- **Its license**: what covers this very file, which may be the pack's license or a rule for the
  part of the pack it is in, with the credit line if one is asked for.
- **Its pack**: which pack, creator, source page and when it was added.
- **What proves it**: the license files and page snapshot kept with the pack.

## Moving around

- **←** and **→** step through the results you are viewing, with a filmstrip along the bottom.
- **F** fits to the window, **1** shows it at actual size.
- **I** hides or shows the details.
- **Space** or **Esc** closes it.

From here you can star it, add it to a collection, link it into a game, open the file in your own
tools, or show it in Finder or Explorer.
