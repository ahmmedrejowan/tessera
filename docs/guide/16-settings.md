# Settings

Two halves: what belongs to the library that is open, and what belongs to Tessera on this computer.

## This library

**General**
- **Name**: what the switcher and the top bar call it.
- **Folder**: where it lives, with a button to show it in Finder or Explorer.
- **Packs Tessera is sure about**: when on, a pack whose license is stated in the download, or set
  by your rule for its site, goes straight into the library. Off sends everything to Review.
- **Close this library**: back to the welcome screen, to open or make another. Backups and sync
  carry on as set.

**Backups** — see [Backups](/docs/backups).

**Sync** — see [Sync](/docs/sync).

**Bin**
- **Keep deleted things for**: a week, a month, or until you empty it yourself.
- **Open the bin**, and empty it.

**Previews and index**
- **Read the library again**: reads every pack from scratch. Useful after moving or editing files
  by hand; Tessera normally notices on its own.
- **Thumbnails**: how much space the drawn previews take, with a button to clear them. They are
  drawn again as you browse.
- **File previews**: build them for this library or every library, retry the ones that failed, or
  stop the work.

## Tessera

**Appearance**
- **Theme**: follow the system, or pin light or dark.
- **Colour**: the accent the window uses.

**Sites**: the rules you have settled for each site, with the license and creator to assume.
Editing or removing one changes what happens to the next pack from that site.

**Downloads**
- **When a download finishes**: add it straight away or hold it.
- **How many at once**.

**AI agents** — see [AI agents](/docs/agents).

**Paired computers**: the computers this one syncs libraries with.

**Helpers**: the small official programs Tessera fetches when you ask for a feature that needs
them: Kopia for backups, rclone for cloud storage, Syncthing for sync. Each shows its version and
where it came from, and can be removed.

**Privacy and problems**
- **Error reports**: never, ask each time, or always. Off by default.
- **Report a problem**: opens an issue with the details already filled in.
- **Logs**: show the log folder, or open today's log.
