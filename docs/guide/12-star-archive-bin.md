# Starred, archive and bin

Three quiet places, each with a different job.

## Starred

Press **S** on anything, or the star in the viewer. Starred things are a personal shortlist: they
sort first, and Browse can show only them. Stars are yours, not a property of the pack.

## Archive

Archiving a pack takes it out of search, out of Browse and out of the way, without deleting a
single file. Use it for packs you are done with but do not want to lose. The Archive page lists
them, and one press puts a pack back.

## Bin

Deleting a pack or a file moves it to the library's bin, which lives inside the library folder and
travels with it. The bin lists what is in it and when it goes, and one press restores anything.

Settings sets how long deleted things are kept: a week, a month, or until you empty it yourself.
**Empty the bin** is the one action in Tessera that cannot be undone, which is why it is also off
by default for AI agents.
